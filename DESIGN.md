## Overview

Agent Hub is independent analysis for developers choosing and operating agent harnesses. Its signature is a readable chronology of work paired with compact comparison facts—not decorative glow. It should feel like a research publication that happens to launch software: calm, factual, and fast to scan.

## Colors

Paper is the canvas and ink establishes hierarchy. Violet, yellow, and orange identify three analytical dimensions: harness capability, throughput, and cost/risk. Green is reserved for confirmed healthy execution.

## Typography

Use Georgia only for the page thesis and key comparison titles. Arial carries controls and explanatory text. Monospace is reserved for paths, digests, commands, and streaming events.

## Layout

Desktop uses a compact horizontal navigation band, a thesis-like page header, an execution canvas, and comparison cards. On narrow screens the navigation scrolls horizontally and panels stack. Keep the run timeline readable at every width.

## Elevation & Depth

Static panels use a pale surface and hairline gray borders rather than shadows. Elevation is reserved for approval dialogs and temporary menus.

## Shapes

Use softly engineered corners; avoid pills for information containers. Status chips are compact rounded rectangles, never floating decoration.

## Components

Buttons preserve their width while busy. Timeline entries always include time, actor, action, and outcome in text. Errors explain the next recovery action.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do use the timeline and comparison cards to make work legible. Do show harness limitations honestly. Do not use generic hero gradients, excessive cards, fake percentage progress, or color alone to communicate state.
